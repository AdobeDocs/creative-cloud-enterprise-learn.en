# Get to your rough cut faster

Learn how to build a rough cut from interviews and B-roll faster in Premiere, without scrubbing through every clip. See how Text-Based Editing lets you pick dialogue from a transcript, and how Media Intelligence finds B-roll by describing what’s on screen, like “hands painting a mural. You still decide what stays, where it cuts, and how the story feels.
