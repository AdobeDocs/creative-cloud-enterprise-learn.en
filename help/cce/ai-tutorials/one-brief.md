# 20 directions from one brief, before lunch

Learn how to turn a creative brief into a shared board of strong visual directions, with generative tools doing the heavy lifting and you making the call. Creative Technologist Mary Poplin shows how to use Firefly Boards to pull in sketches and references, generate a batch of options, edit down the ideas, and narrow to the two or three directions worth defending.
