# New in Adobe Premiere: Pull dialogue, ambience, and noise apart

That windy rooftop interview is hard to hear. You don’t have to cut it. Learn how to rescue interview audio recorded in the wind with the AI-enhanced tools in Premiere’s Essential Sound panel. Pull the voice forward and reduce background noise, enhance the voice track, then finish your edit with the clip you have.
